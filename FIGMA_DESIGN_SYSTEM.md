## 2. Typography Scale

* **Display Font (Brand Logo & Insignia):** `Cinzel`, Serif (Uppercase, Letter-Spacing: 0.05em)
* **Prestige Editorial Font (Headings):** `Playfair Display`, Serif (Bold 700 / SemiBold 600)
* **Corporate Body & UI Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, Sans-Serif (Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700)

### Typographic Hierarchy:
* **H1 Hero Headline:** `3.6rem` (58px), Line-height: `1.15`, Playfair Display
* **H2 Section Titles:** `2.85rem` (46px), Line-height: `1.25`, Playfair Display
* **H3 Card Titles:** `1.25rem` (20px), SemiBold, Playfair Display
* **Body Regular:** `1.0rem` (16px), Line-height: `1.75`, Plus Jakarta Sans
* **Badges & Overlines:** `0.825rem` (13px), All-Caps, SemiBold 600
