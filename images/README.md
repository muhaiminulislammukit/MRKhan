# M. R. KHAN & ASSOCIATES — Image Assets Guide
**Folder Location:** `rakib khan/images/`

This folder contains all images used across the website, organized neatly into dedicated categories so you can easily replace, update, or add your own photos anytime.

---

## 📁 Folder Structure & File Explanations

### 1. `images/profile/` (Your Profile & Headshots)
* **File:** `rakib_khan_portrait.jpg`
* **Used in:** Homepage Hero Section & Header.
* **Recommended Dimensions:** `800 x 800` px or `1000 x 1000` px (Square or 4:5 aspect ratio).
* **How to replace:** Rename your professional photo to `rakib_khan_portrait.jpg` and drop it into this `profile/` folder. It will instantly update on your website!

### 2. `images/chambers/` (Law Firm Office & Chambers)
* **File:** `law_firm_office.jpg`
* **Used in:** "About Advocate Rakib Khan" Section & Firm Overview.
* **Recommended Dimensions:** `1920 x 1080` px (16:9 Landscape).
* **How to replace:** Take a photo of your Banasree office, conference table, or law library, rename it to `law_firm_office.jpg`, and drop it here.

### 3. `images/credentials/` (NBR Permit, Bar License, Degrees)
* **File:** `credential_tax_permit.jpg`
* **Used in:** Academic & Professional Licensing Section.
* **Recommended Dimensions:** `800 x 600` px or `1200 x 900` px (4:3 aspect ratio).
* **How to replace:** Scan or take a photo of your official Income Tax Practitioner permit or certificate from NBR, rename to `credential_tax_permit.jpg`, and replace this file.

### 4. `images/cases/` (Portfolio & Work Case Studies)
* **Files:**
  - `case_tax_tribunal.jpg`: NBR Taxes Appellate Tribunal & Tax Appeal representation.
  - `case_rjsc_corporate.jpg`: Company registration, foreign subsidiary, and BIDA work.
  - `case_foreign_fund.jpg`: Multilateral development finance (ADB, World Bank, IDCOL solar/infrastructure).
* **Recommended Dimensions:** `800 x 500` px (16:10 or 16:9 Landscape).
* **How to replace:** Replace with real project photos, signing ceremonies, or client engagement images.

### 5. `images/branding/` (Logo, Seals & Watermarks)
* Reserved for your custom vector logo (`logo.png`, `logo.svg`), firm seal, or letterhead crest.

---

## 💡 Quick Tips for Changing Images
1. Keep the exact same filenames (e.g. `rakib_khan_portrait.jpg`) so you do not even need to edit the HTML code!
2. Supported image extensions: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`.
3. To compress large photos for fast website loading, use free tools like [TinyPNG](https://tinypng.com).
